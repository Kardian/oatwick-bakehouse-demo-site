Our story

A corner shop, a jar of starter and an early alarm.

IMAGE: The dark green shopfront of the bakery on a brick terraced street, with bread on shelves in the window

Oatwick started in 2019 when Mara took over the empty corner shop on Mercer Row. The shelves were still there from the old grocer. She painted the front green, put in one deck oven and opened with three kinds of bread.

The starter is older than the shop. It lives in a glass jar on the bench and gets fed with flour and water every morning before anything else happens.

How we bake

  1. Thursday: mix the dough and let it rest overnight.
  2. Friday: fold, shape by hand, and proof slowly in the cold.
  3. Before sunrise: bake, cool, and stack the shelves by 7:00.
IMAGE: A glass jar of bubbly sourdough starter next to a paper bag of flour and a wooden spoon

What goes in

Flour, water, salt and time. Our pastries add butter, sugar and cardamom. Nothing else needs to be on the label, so nothing else is in the bread.

Whatever we have left at closing goes to the community fridge two streets over.
